# /friday-brief

Read `.claude/active10-context.md` first. Week = Monday through today.

## Sections

1. **Revenue** — wholesale (`orders` total, non-cancelled) + retail (QB sales by product for the
   week) vs last week and vs the 4-week average. One line per channel with the % change.
2. **Top sellers** — top 5 products by units across both channels; call out any product that
   moved ±30%.
3. **Wins** — new wholesale accounts (first order this week), applications approved, prospects
   that replied or converted, winback codes redeemed (`winback_offers.redeemed_at`, with
   `revenue_recovered`), referrals qualified, big orders (> $500).
4. **Watch next week** — invoices about to go 30+ days overdue, customers past their usual
   reorder date (see `/call-list`), low engagement in outreach (reply rate dropping), products
   with stock concerns Darrin has mentioned, anything unanswered in Gmail > 48h from a customer.

## Output

Short brief in chat, numbers with sources. Active 10 has no Slack — if Darrin wants it
shared, create a Gmail draft (subject `Friday brief — <date>`) addressed as he says.
